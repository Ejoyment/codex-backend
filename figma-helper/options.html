How syncing works

  1. Open Buildrs and sign in. The helper reads your Buildrs token from the page.
  2. Keep a signed-in figma.com tab available.
  3. Click Sync now. Your Figma recent files appear on the Buildrs Figma page automatically.

Only file keys, names, thumbnails and modified dates are sent to Buildrs. Your Figma cookies and password never leave your browser.
